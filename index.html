Audit path

From application to certificate

Initial certification follows a fixed sequence. Surveillance audits follow during the three-year cycle.

  1. Application

    You send scope, sites, headcount and the standard. We confirm audit time and fees.

  2. Readiness audit

    The auditor checks documented information and whether your system is ready.

  3. Implementation audit

    The auditor samples records, interviews staff and observes work against the standard.

  4. Nonconformities

    Findings are agreed, corrected and verified before the file goes to review.

  5. Decision

    An independent reviewer decides. If approved, the certificate is issued and listed in the register.

Questions

Before you apply

What is ISO certification?

It confirms that an audit found your management system meets the requirements of a standard. ISO publishes the standards. Certification bodies such as Kab International carry out the audits.

How long does certification take?

It depends on how ready your system is, your size, the number of sites and how quickly findings are closed. We give an audit plan after reviewing your application.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the standard, employee numbers, sites and scope. We quote after reviewing your registration.

Do you also write my management system?

No. We do not consult on systems we certify. You can use an independent consultant or build the system in-house.

How do I enrol in a training course?

Open the Training page, choose a course and press Enrol. Your details are prepared as a WhatsApp message to our training desk.
